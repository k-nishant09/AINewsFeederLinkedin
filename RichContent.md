## The One Sentence That Matters Most

**This is not a LinkedIn post generator. This is a live broadcast media round-table program in text.**

The reader should feel like they are sitting in an editorial room watching an insightful journalist host a lively, grounded debate between four distinct human perspectives (Founder, Policy Analyst, Engineer, Generalist). If it reads like a dry bullet-point press release, the format has failed.

## Part 1 — The Anatomy of the Round-Table Post

Every AIFeeders post is assembled with strict broadcast pacing:

```text
1. 🎙️ Media Host Opening & Hook
   (Curiosity-first narrative frame — immediately poses the core question)

2. Situation & Human Analogy
   (Grounds the news event in everyday intuitive physics / architecture)

3. Dynamic Panelist Transitions & Dialogue Exchange
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   [Host introduces the policy & governance implications]
   🏛️ Policy Analyst: "..."

   [Host brings in the founder on commercial risk & capital]
   💼 Founder: "..."

   [Host pivots to the engineer on deployment reality & security]
   🧠 Engineer: "..."

   [Host connects to the generalist on human scale & trust]
   🎓 Generalist: "..."
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4. 🎙️ Media Host Synthesis
   (Extracts second-order consequences and deep structural meaning)

5. Full Story Source Attribution Link

6. 🎙️ Media Host Audience CTA Dilemma
   (Open, balanced dilemma inviting senior practitioner debate)

7. Disclaimer & Dynamic SEO/AEO Hashtags (100% Entity-Derived)
```

## Part 2 — The 4 Core Roundtable Personas

| Persona | Primary Mental Model | What They Question | Voice Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **💼 Founder** | Unit economics, customer acquisition cost, platform lock-in, moat defensibility | "Can this generate sustainable margin or is it a commoditized wrapper?" | Decisive, practical, grounded in fundraising and operational P&L |
| **🏛️ Policy Analyst** | EU AI Act, liability boundaries, audit rights, data sovereignty, public trust | "Who carries the legal liability when this system makes an ungrounded decision?" | Precise, structured, referencing real-world governance frameworks |
| **🧠 Engineer** | Architecture, security perimeters, observability, failure modes, integration friction | "How do we monitor and rollback this agent in a zero-downtime production pipeline?" | Direct, pragmatic, skeptical of marketing benchmarks |
| **🎓 Generalist** | Everyday human utility, career transitions, cognitive load, workplace reality | "What does this actually change for someone outside the tech bubble on Monday morning?" | Accessible, empathetic, zero corporate buzzwords |
